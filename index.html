Why This Matters

Somewhere in Chicago tonight, a mother is quietly doing the math: rent, the heating bill, and the holidays that are coming whether she’s ready or not. Her kids have already learned not to ask for much. A man who used to work just a few blocks from here is sleeping under a viaduct, in shoes worn through by the first snow.

These aren’t strangers. They’re our neighbors. They ride the same trains, walk the same streets, and love their families just as fiercely as we love ours.

The holidays are supposed to feel like warmth. For too many families in our city, they feel like one more thing they can’t afford.

This season, we’re changing that, one turkey, one coat, one pair of shoes, one wrapped gift at a time. Every dollar you give becomes something real in someone’s hands: a Thanksgiving dinner that brings a family back to the same table, a coat that makes a Chicago January survivable, a present that tells a child someone thought of you.

We can’t do it alone. With you, we can do something beautiful.

How We Distribute in Chicago

Your gift goes straight from our hands to our neighbors’. No middlemen.

  1. We Buy in Bulk

    Donations are pooled to buy turkeys, coats, shoes and toys at the lowest possible prices, and stretched further with in-kind donations from local businesses.

  2. We Partner Locally

    We work with community centers, churches, schools and shelters to find the families and individuals who need help most.

  3. We Show Up

    Volunteers hand out meals and gifts at community events and bring warm clothes directly to people living on the street.

  4. We Show You

    We’ll post photos, receipts and updates on our fundraiser page so you can see exactly where your money went.

Main distribution hub: 7114 S Vincennes Ave, Chicago, IL 60621 (Englewood)

Giving Safely Matters

There are too many fake fundraisers out there. Here’s exactly how you can check us out before you give a single dollar.

Secure Donations Only

Every donation goes through our verified GoFundMe campaign — a secure platform with donor protection. We never see your card number, and we never collect cash, wire transfers, gift cards, or payment apps.

Receipts & Photo Updates

After every distribution we post photos, receipts, and a full breakdown of what was spent where — right on our fundraiser page and this site. You give it, you see it.

Talk to a Real Person

Questions before you give? Call or text (212) 742-2301 or email chicagoholidayhope@gmail.com. We answer.

Questions

Is this fundraiser legitimate?

Yes — and we want you to check us out. Every donation is processed by GoFundMe, a platform with its own fraud protections and refund policies. We publish receipts, photos, and spending breakdowns after every distribution, and you can call or text us directly at (212) 742-2301 with any question before or after you give.

How do I know my donation is safe?

Your payment never touches us. You donate on GoFundMe’s secure, encrypted checkout — we never see your card details. We also never ask for cash, wire transfers, gift cards, Venmo/Zelle/CashApp, or any unusual payment method. If anyone claiming to represent us asks for those, it is a scam. Report it to us right away.

Where does my money go?

Directly to turkeys and holiday meals, coats and warm clothing, shoes, and gifts for kids in Chicago. We’ll share updates and photos on our fundraiser page.

How do I donate?

Click any “Donate” button. You’ll be taken to our secure fundraiser page. We never collect card details on this site.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

This is a community-led fundraiser. Unless and until a registered nonprofit fiscal sponsor is listed here, donations should not be considered tax-deductible. [Update if you partner with a registered nonprofit]

Who is organizing this?

The Chicagons — a family of blessed Chicago neighbors who believe nobody in our city should spend the holidays cold, hungry, or forgotten.

Can my company sponsor or match?

Yes. See sponsor levels above, or contact chicagoholidayhope@gmail.com. Many employers also match personal gifts.

What happens if you raise more than $80,000?

Every extra dollar goes to more families: more meals, more coats, and more gifts, and anything left continues into winter support.
